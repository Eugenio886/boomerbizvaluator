Owner Financing Reality Check

Many profitable businesses never sell — not because they’re bad businesses, but because buyers can’t qualify for bank financing.

In those cases, owner financing is often the only viable exit.

During your valuation session, we look at whether owner financing is realistic for your business, what terms buyers actually accept, and how structure impacts both price and risk.
